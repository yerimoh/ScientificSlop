Most sentences in an introduction are NOT key claims. Answer "none" if the sentence is any of these:
  * background about a field or a problem
  * a description or definition of how some method works
  * an announcement of what this paper does, proposes, introduces or demonstrates
  * a statement of contributions, or a pointer to a later section, table or figure
  * a report of this paper's own results

A sentence is a key claim ONLY if it asserts something contestable that a careful reader would
demand a justification for, in one of these three senses:
  superiority       asserts that this paper's method is better than or preferable to a named alternative
  prior_limitation  asserts that a named prior method, or a class of prior methods, falls short
  design_choice     gives the reason WHY a component, setting or approach was chosen

When in doubt, answer none.
